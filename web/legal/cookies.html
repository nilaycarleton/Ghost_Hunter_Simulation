Cookie Notice

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Essential Storage

The site may use essential cookies or equivalent browser storage for authentication sessions, account state, security, and replay access. These are needed for signed-in features.

Local Preferences

The light/dark theme setting is stored in localStorage as haunted-theme. Clearing browser site data removes this preference.

Analytics and Advertising

This implementation does not include advertising cookies or targeted-ad tracking. If analytics or advertising tools are added later, update this notice before deployment.

Managing Cookies

You can block, delete, or restrict cookies in your browser settings. Some signed-in features may stop working if essential authentication storage is disabled.
